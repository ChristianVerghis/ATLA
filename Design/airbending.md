# Airbending — implemented spec (designed inline 2026-08-13)

Martial basis: **Baguazhang** — circular, evasive, never meeting force with force. The mobility element: canon's most defensive art; offense is *displacement*, not damage. Runs on **chi** with the cheapest costs and lightest hits.

| Technique | Input | Chi | CD | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Air Blast | LMB (hold = auto) | 5 | 0.35s | 8 dmg palm bolt + 700 knockback — pushes, barely hurts |
| Air Swipe | Q | 10 | 1.2s | Wide flat crescent, 12 dmg + 950 knockback (crowd sweep) |
| Wind Dome | E | 20 | 6s | 4s mobile shield that shreds incoming enemy projectiles |
| Cyclone | R | 40 | 12s | Tornado at aim point: lifts, spins, 15 dmg (Aang's air vortex) |
| Air Scooter | F | 20 | 6s | 4s riding a spinning wind ball — the bender is lifted onto it and pirouettes on top, show-style, at ×1.8 speed |
| Updraft | RMB hold | 8/s | — | Feather-fall glide: gravity 0.15, fall capped — leap from anywhere |

Passives while attuned: jump 720 (vs 560) and 0.9 air control — airbenders live above the fight. Home zone: sky temple with floating platforms at 2.6m–9m that only air can comfortably traverse (scooter + jump + updraft). Counterplay: the dome blanks projectile elements but not earth's sliding wall or fire's nova; air wins by refusing engagements, loses attrition wars.

**Look (v2, 2026-08-14):** active air is clearly visible (M_AirBand): blast bolts corkscrew with orbiting wind streaks, impacts burst as flat gusts, the dome whirls with thin spinning bands, the cyclone funnel is thicker, and every cast converges wisps from a ring around the body into the hand.
